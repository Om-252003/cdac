### **1. Which component of information theory is used to measure uncertainty in data?**  
**Correct Answer: B**  
- **A) Variance** – ❌ Measures dispersion, not information uncertainty  
- **B) Entropy** – ✅ Quantifies uncertainty in information theory  
- **C) Mean** – ❌ Central tendency only  
- **D) Standard deviation** – ❌ Spread of data, not uncertainty  

---

### **2. Probably Approximately Correct (PAC) learning provides guarantees about:**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Zero training error** – ❌ PAC allows approximation  
- **B) Exact hypothesis recovery** – ❌ Exact learning not required  
- **C) Performance with high probability** – ✅ Core PAC guarantee  
- **D) Infinite data availability** – ❌ Finite samples assumed  

---

### **3. Which learning paradigm learns by receiving rewards and penalties?**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Supervised learning** – ❌ Uses labeled data  
- **B) Unsupervised learning** – ❌ No feedback  
- **C) Reinforcement learning** – ✅ Reward–penalty based  
- **D) Semi-supervised learning** – ❌ Label mix, not reward  

---

### **4. Which tradeoff describes conflict between model simplicity and accuracy?**  
**Correct Answer: B**  
- **A) Precision–Recall tradeoff** – ❌ Classification metric tradeoff  
- **B) Bias–Variance tradeoff** – ✅ Simplicity vs complexity  
- **C) Exploration–Exploitation tradeoff** – ❌ RL concept  
- **D) Time–Space tradeoff** – ❌ Algorithm design
