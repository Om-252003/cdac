# 📘 CDAC MOCK TEST – 7 (Full Elimination Notes)

## ✅ ANSWERS & FULL ELIMINATION

---

### **1. Which statement best explains overfitting?**  
**Correct Answer: B**  
- **A) Model performs well on unseen data** – ❌ That indicates good generalization  
- **B) Model captures noise instead of signal** – ✅ Core definition of overfitting  
- **C) Model has high bias** – ❌ Overfitting relates to high variance  
- **D) Model is too simple** – ❌ Leads to underfitting  

---

### **2. Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM) focuses on minimizing:**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Test error** – ❌ Not available during training  
- **B) Structural risk** – ❌ SRM concept  
- **C) Training error** – ✅ ERM definition  
- **D) Model complexity** – ❌ Ignored in ERM  

---

### **3. Which factor directly controls hypothesis space size?**  
**Correct Answer: B**  
- **A) Learning rate** – ❌ Optimization parameter  
- **B) VC dimension** – ✅ Capacity measure  
- **C) Dataset size** – ❌ Data quantity  
- **D) Loss function** – ❌ Optimization criterion  

---

### **4. Which ML algorithm is most robust to irrelevant features?**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) KNN** – ❌ Distance polluted by irrelevant features  
- **B) Linear Regression** – ❌ Coefficients affected  
- **C) Decision Tree** – ✅ Feature selection via splits  
- **D) K-means** – ❌ Distance-based  

---

### **5. Which distance metric is best for text vectors (TF-IDF)?**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Euclidean** – ❌ Magnitude sensitive  
- **B) Manhattan** – ❌ Scale sensitive  
- **C) Cosine similarity** – ✅ Direction-based  
- **D) Hamming** – ❌ Binary only
