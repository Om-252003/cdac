### **1. Which of the following best explains the bias–variance tradeoff?**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Increasing model complexity always improves performance** – ❌ Leads to overfitting  
- **B) High bias models perform well on training data** – ❌ High bias underfits  
- **C) High variance models generalize poorly to unseen data** – ✅ Core idea of variance  
- **D) Bias and variance are independent of model complexity** – ❌ They depend on complexity  

---

### **2. Which learning framework assumes that the hypothesis space can be ordered by increasing complexity?**  
**Correct Answer: B**  
- **A) Empirical Risk Minimization** – ❌ Considers training error only  
- **B) Structural Risk Minimization** – ✅ Explicitly orders hypotheses by complexity  
- **C) PAC Learning** – ❌ General learning framework  
- **D) Cross Validation** – ❌ Model evaluation technique  

---

### **3. Occam’s Razor in machine learning suggests that:**  
**Correct Answer: B**  
- **A) The most complex model should always be chosen** – ❌ Opposite of Occam  
- **B) Simpler hypotheses are preferred if performance is similar** – ✅ Core principle  
- **C) Models with more parameters generalize better** – ❌ Overfitting risk  
- **D) Simplicity guarantees zero error** – ❌ No such guarantee
