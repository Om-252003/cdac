### **1. Which of the following best explains Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM)?**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Minimizing validation error** – ❌ Validation data is not used in ERM  
- **B) Minimizing expected risk** – ❌ Expected risk is theoretical, not computable  
- **C) Minimizing training error** – ✅ ERM directly minimizes empirical (training) loss  
- **D) Minimizing test error** – ❌ Test data is unseen during training  

---

### **2. Structural Risk Minimization primarily helps in controlling:**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Bias only** – ❌ Bias alone is not controlled  
- **B) Variance only** – ❌ Variance alone is not controlled  
- **C) Model complexity** – ✅ SRM balances bias–variance via complexity control  
- **D) Training data size** – ❌ Data size is unchanged  

---

### **3. Which scenario best represents high bias?**  
**Correct Answer: B**  
- **A) Low training error, high test error** – ❌ Indicates overfitting  
- **B) High training error, high test error** – ✅ Underfitting → high bias  
- **C) Low training error, low test error** – ❌ Ideal case  
- **D) Random performance** – ❌ No learning pattern  

---

### **4. VC dimension is mainly used to measure:**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Dataset size** – ❌ Independent of VC dimension  
- **B) Feature count** – ❌ VC ≠ number of features  
- **C) Model capacity** – ✅ VC dimension measures hypothesis capacity  
- **D) Optimization speed** – ❌ Not related  

---

### **5. Which statement about No Free Lunch Theorem is TRUE?**  
**Correct Answer: C**  
- **A) Some algorithms always outperform others** – ❌ False  
- **B) One model fits all problems** – ❌ Contradicts NFL  
- **C) No single algorithm works best for all problems** – ✅ Core NFL principle  
- **D) Deep learning is universally superior** – ❌ No universal best  

---

### **6. Which distance metric is most suitable for text represented using TF-IDF?**

- **B) Manhattan distance** – ❌ Not direction-aware  
- **C) Cosine similarity** – ✅ Direction matters in text vectors  
- **D) Hamming distance** – ❌ For categorical/binary data
